Keeping Real Support Free for All Teens

Facts Only was created because we believe every teen deserves authentic mental health support - not corporate wellness speak or toxic positivity.

Too many teens struggle with confidence, social anxiety, and communication skills but can't access helpful resources. Therapy is expensive, school counselors are overwhelmed, and most apps either treat teens like children or push them toward crisis services.

We built Facts Only to bridge that gap - providing genuine, evidence-based support wrapped in language teens actually connect with. Our AI personalities aren't trying to replace therapy; they're here to help with the everyday confidence and communication challenges that teens face.

Our Promise: Facts Only will always be free. We believe financial barriers shouldn't prevent teens from accessing mental health support.
